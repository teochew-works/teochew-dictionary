# ADR-0016: Reject TTS Synthesis as an Audio Supplement

## Status

✅ Accepted

## Context

Audio coverage is the dataset's most visible gap: most Chaozhou syllables and all of
Shantou/Chaoyang remain unrecorded, and every unrecorded syllable resolves to `null` in the built
artifact. Volunteer recordings fill that gap slowly and depend on finding speakers.

Text-to-speech is the obvious accelerator. This dictionary already derives IPA for every syllable
(ADR-0001), so on the face of it the input a synthesiser needs is already there. Issue #58 asked
whether synthesis could supplement recordings.

## Decision

*Amended 2026-09-11 by [ADR-0027](adr-0027.md):* the rejection below is of *generating* Teochew
audio — TTS, a phoneme profile, or any synthesis of a syllable with no recording — and it stands.
ADR-0027 permits one narrower thing it does not cover: re-rendering the speaker's *own* recordings
toward that speaker's own measured per-tone medians, published as a separate, labelled,
confidence-capped speaker tier beside the untouched originals.

We will **not** synthesize audio. Surveyed in
[data/phonology/TTS.md](../../data/phonology/TTS.md) (issue #58, 2026-08-17) and rejected, on two
findings:

- **No commercial TTS platform has a Teochew or Southern Min voice**, and none offers a way to
  carry this dataset's Chao-numeral tone contours. Tone is not a prosodic garnish in Teochew — it
  is lexically contrastive, and a synthesiser that cannot reproduce the contour produces a
  different word, or no word.
- **The one technically viable path is a from-scratch eSpeak-NG phoneme profile.** That is real
  linguistics work, and it is not worth it against a recording gap that is already small and
  bounded — the syllable inventory is a few hundred entries per variety (ADR-0010), not an open set.

The gap is closed by recording instead: issue #106 imported Lingua Libre/Commons clips, and issue
#128 (ADR-0017) added a record button so a contributor at a microphone can capture a syllable
directly. Together those took Chaozhou from zero clips to more than 1,900.

## Consequences

- Effort went into the recording pipeline — import, browser capture, staging, merge — rather than
  into a synthesis profile, and that pipeline produced real audio from real speakers.
- **Every clip in the dataset is either a human recording or — since
  [ADR-0027](adr-0027.md) — a labelled, provenance-linked resynthesis of one**, which matters for a
  language whose descriptions disagree with each other (ADR-0003): a clip *generated* from this
  project's own provisional analysis would present that analysis as evidence for itself.
  Recordings are independent evidence; a derived tier renders toward the speaker's measured
  production, not the analysis, and says what it is.
- Negative: coverage grows only as fast as speakers are found, and Shantou and Chaoyang (issue #37)
  still have no clips at all.
- Neutral: this records a *non*-implementation. If a Southern Min voice with contour control
  becomes available, or if the eSpeak-NG profile becomes cheap, the survey in `TTS.md` is the
  starting point for revisiting it — the decision rests on the state of the tooling, not on a
  principle against synthesis.

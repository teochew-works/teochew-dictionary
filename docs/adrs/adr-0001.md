# ADR-0001: Store Peng'im Only; Derive IPA, POJ and Sandhi

## Status

✅ Accepted

## Context

A Teochew lexicon needs to present a reading in several notations. Contributors and learners in
the Chaoshan diaspora use **Peng'im** (拼音, the 1960 Guangdong scheme); linguists and anyone
comparing across topolects want **IPA**; anyone coming from Hokkien materials expects **POJ**.
Tone sandhi — which changes the surface tone of every non-final syllable — is a fourth form of
the same reading.

The obvious data model is to give an entry parallel columns: `pengim:`, `ipa:`, `poj:`,
`sandhi:`. It is also what most hand-built dictionary datasets do.

Three forces argue against it:

1. Parallel hand-maintained columns drift. A contributor correcting a tone in one column has no
   mechanism forcing the other three to follow.
2. A phonological correction — "`e` is /ɯ/, not /e/" — is a change to the *language description*,
   not to any one entry, but with stored IPA it becomes an edit to every affected entry.
3. **IPA cannot be variety-neutral.** 潮 is [tie⁵⁵] in Chaozhou city and [tio⁵⁵] in Shantou, for
   the same headword and the same Peng'im. A single stored `ipa:` field would have to silently
   pick a variety and pretend the choice wasn't made.

## Decision

We will store **Peng'im as the only romanisation in the source data**. IPA, POJ and sandhi forms
are derived at build time from the phonology tables in `data/phonology/`.

An entry records `readings[].pengim` and a `variety`; the build
([src/build/enrich.ts](../../src/build/enrich.ts), over
[src/phonology/](../../src/phonology/)) parses the syllable
([syllable.ts](../../src/phonology/syllable.ts)), composes IPA from the variety table
([ipa.ts](../../src/phonology/ipa.ts)), transliterates POJ ([poj.ts](../../src/phonology/poj.ts)),
and applies the sandhi rules ([sandhi.ts](../../src/phonology/sandhi.ts)), emitting `ipa`, `poj`,
`sandhi`, `ipa_confidence` and `ipa_caveats` onto every reading in `dist/dict.json`.

Hand-written `ipa:` / `poj:` overrides remain available for genuinely irregular forms, but the
validator requires a `note` justifying each one, so an override is a documented exception rather
than a quiet fork of the derivation.

The validator (`npm run validate`) enforces that every Peng'im syllable parses against the
orthography in [data/phonology/pengim.yaml](../../data/phonology/pengim.yaml) and that IPA and POJ
derivation actually succeeds for every reading — so a reading that the derivation cannot handle
fails the build rather than shipping an empty field.

## Consequences

- **A contributor writes one romanisation, not four.** The barrier to adding an entry is knowing
  Teochew, not knowing IPA.
- **Fixing the phonology fixes the whole dictionary.** Correcting a vowel or tone mapping in one
  YAML file re-derives every affected entry, and the change is reviewable as a single small diff
  rather than a thousand-line data churn.
- The orthography becomes load-bearing, which surfaces a whole class of errors mechanically: the
  validator can check that checked tones (4, 8) co-occur with a stop coda (`-b -g -h`) and that
  the other six forbid one, catching slips that a free-text IPA column would absorb silently.
- Negative: the derivation is only as good as the tables. Where the analysis is unsettled, the
  derived form is uncertain — which is why every mapping carries a confidence (ADR-0003).
- Negative: a genuinely irregular reading costs more than it would in a hand-written model — an
  override plus a justifying note.
- Enables ADR-0002 (varieties as overlays): once IPA is derived rather than stored, a second
  variety is a table, not a second copy of the lexicon.

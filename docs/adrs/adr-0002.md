# ADR-0002: A Reference Variety with Sparse Overlays

## Status

✅ Accepted

## Context

"Teochew" is not one accent. Chaozhou city, Shantou and the Southern Teochew of Chaoyang differ in
ways this dataset has to represent — most visibly the `io` → [ie] fronting that makes 潮 [tie⁵⁵] in
Chaozhou and [tio⁵⁵] in Shantou, and the /ɯ/ merger that is Southern Teochew's, not Shantou's
(corrected during issue #2).

ADR-0001 makes IPA derived, so a variety is a *table*, not a second lexicon. But the tables
themselves could be structured two ways: each variety a complete, self-contained Peng'im → IPA
mapping, or one full reference variety plus files listing only the differences.

Complete per-variety tables are simpler to read in isolation. They also mean that a correction to
a mapping the varieties agree on has to be applied N times, and that answering "how does Shantou
actually differ from Chaozhou?" requires diffing two large files.

## Decision

We will treat **Chaozhou as the reference variety** and express every other variety as a **sparse
overlay** listing only what differs.

[data/phonology/varieties/chaozhou.yaml](../../data/phonology/varieties/chaozhou.yaml) is the
complete Peng'im → IPA mapping. [shantou.yaml](../../data/phonology/varieties/shantou.yaml) and
[chaoyang.yaml](../../data/phonology/varieties/chaoyang.yaml) (issue #10, 2026-08-03) carry only
their own divergences and inherit everything else. Variety ids are filesystem-derived
(`listVarieties()` in [src/phonology/load.ts](../../src/phonology/load.ts)), so adding a variety is
adding a file.

An entry's `readings[].variety` selects which overlay applies; `DEFAULT_VARIETY`
([src/schema/entry.ts](../../src/schema/entry.ts)) supplies it when a reading doesn't say.

## Consequences

- **The overlay file *is* the description of the dialect difference.** `shantou.yaml` read on its
  own answers "what makes Shantou Shantou", which no pair of complete tables does.
- A correction to shared phonology is one edit, and it propagates to every variety that hasn't
  deliberately overridden it.
- Adding a variety is cheap — a file of differences, not a full transcription of the syllabary.
  This is what made Chaoyang (issue #10) a small change rather than a project.
- Negative: Chaozhou is privileged by construction. A future variety that diverges from Chaozhou
  more than it agrees would be expressed awkwardly as an overlay, and the reference choice would
  have to be revisited.
- Negative: reading a non-reference variety's real mapping means resolving two files, not one.
  Mitigated by the derivation being mechanical and by `dist/dict.json` carrying the resolved
  result.
- The variety ids being filesystem-derived means they cannot be a static `z.enum` in the Zod
  schema; they are injected into the emitted JSON Schema instead (see ADR-0004 and
  `entryFileJsonSchema` in [src/schema/emit.ts](../../src/schema/emit.ts)).
